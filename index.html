About

A little about me

I am a Bachelor of Management Studies student at IIM Sirmaur, and I am also doing an online BBA in Digital Business and Entrepreneurship at IIM Bangalore. I am most interested in business strategy and operations, and in understanding how consumers make choices.

Most of my academic work so far has been research and presentation: comparing online and offline shopping channels, estimating the drivers of growth across four economies in Excel, and doing fieldwork on informal housing in Paonta Sahib. I enjoy taking a large, messy question and turning it into something a reader can follow.

On campus, I serve on the Infrastructure & IT Committee, act as a hostel discipline coordinator for about 120 residents, and help with logistics for around 20 college events. Outside college, I mentor students in Prayagraj and teach Hindi online.

I like combining analytical thinking with creativity, whether that means a data-backed recommendation or an AI-generated video campaign. I am now looking for an internship where I can put both to use.
